Home/How it works

The book is how we start.
Belonging is the point.

We send book clubs to women navigating financial hardship, recovery, domestic violence, illness, and grief — the seasons that isolate. The book gives them something to talk about. The circle gives them somewhere to belong. Here is what a book does for a woman in a hard season, and what we put in her hands.

What a book does

Why we start with a book, and not with a program.

Recognition
A woman in a hard season opens a book and finds someone on the page carrying something like it. Nothing about her circumstances has changed, but the belief that she is the only one has taken its first real hit.
Something to say
In most rooms she enters, the subject is her situation. In this one it is a character, a choice, an ending she disagreed with. For two hours she is a reader with an opinion rather than a case with a file.
A wider world
Stories put a woman somewhere she has not been, in a life she has not lived, and hand her back her own with the edges moved. That is how a book quietly changes what she believes is available to her.
Hers to keep
The book does not come back. It goes home, onto a shelf or a nightstand, and stays there as evidence that someone chose it for her and that she finished it.
The circle
The book runs out; the women do not. What lasts is the room — the ones who showed up week after week, learned each other’s names, and now have a reason to keep asking after one another.

What we provide

Everything in the carton, at no cost to your organization or to the women in your group.

Books
One copy per woman, hers to keep.
Journals & pens
One of each per woman, from women-owned makers.
Women-owned business card
A printed card naming the women-owned businesses her kit came from.
Facilitator guide
Welcome script, discussion questions, journal prompts.
Delivery
Hand-delivered locally in Colorado, shipped elsewhere.
Someone to ask
A person you can email when something comes up.

Questions we get asked

What does it cost?
Nothing. Not to your organization, and not to the women who receive a kit. The program is funded by donations and, in time, by paid boxes bought by people who can afford them.
Do we give the books back?
No. The book, the journal, the pen and the card belong to the woman who receives them.
How do I donate?
Through the Donate page, once or monthly. Card giving runs through Stripe and goes live shortly; until then an email to Jamie sets it up, and she can send a mailing address if you would rather write a check. We are a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, so gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
What if my group is bigger or smaller than ten?
Tell us. Ten is our standard carton, not a rule.
Can the group meet only once?
Yes. A single gathering is a real book club. Many of our partner settings cannot commit to a series, and that is fine.
Are you outside Colorado?
Colorado and Nebraska are where our founder splits her time, so those are easiest first. Ask anyway — shipping is a cost, not an obstacle.
Some of the material is emotionally heavy. Is that a problem?
Books raise things. We are not a therapy service and we do not pretend to be, and our facilitator agreement says so plainly. We choose titles with your group in mind and you always have final say on the book.
How long does it take to get a carton?
About two weeks, from the conversation where we settle on a book to the carton arriving.
