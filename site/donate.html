Every box begins
a conversation.

A gift buys a book by a woman author, a journal and pen from a woman-owned maker, a card naming the women-owned businesses her kit came from, and the guide that lets any facilitator run the whole club. Then it buys nine more.

Our tax status.

Gathered Pages Collective is a Colorado nonprofit corporation, incorporated 10 June 2026, EIN 42-3092238. The IRS has recognized us as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization, so your gift is tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

What a gift buys Planning ranges, per woman
A pen
$6.75 · A gel pen from Ruff House Print Shop, and the small pleasure of writing with something nicer than you expected.
A journal
$7.30 – $20.27 · The spread is real: the journals come from three different women artists, and we pay each of them her price.
A book
$20.20 – $21.85 · Bought at near retail from a woman-owned bookshop rather than wholesale from a warehouse.
One woman’s kit
About $40 – $55 · The book, the journal and the pen, plus the women-owned business card, bookmark, letter and packaging around them.
A whole club
About $400 – $550 · Ten kits, one carton, one facilitator, one group of women who did not know each other last month.
Give once Give monthly

These are prices we have actually paid, taken from our own expense log — not giving tiers. Give any amount; nothing is too small.
