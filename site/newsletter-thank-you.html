You’re On The List

That is the whole sign-up. The Monthly Gathering comes from Jamie once a month — where the boxes went, what the circles are reading, and the woman-owned business inside that month’s box.

What Happens Next

In a few minutes
A short note from Jamie lands in your inbox, explaining what Gathered Pages does and how a box reaches a woman who needs one.
Tomorrow
This month’s letter follows, so you are not waiting until the next one goes out.
After that
One letter a month, usually less. Every one carries an unsubscribe link that works, and we never sell or share the list.

If it is not there in a few minutes, look in your Promotions tab or your spam folder. Dragging it into your main inbox once tells your email provider the letter is wanted, and the next one will find you properly.
