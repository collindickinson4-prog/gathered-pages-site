Thank You

Your gift is on its way to a woman who will open a box, find a book chosen for her, and sit down in a room with others who are reading it too.

What Happens Next

Your receipt
Stripe emails it to you within a few minutes. It is your record for tax purposes — keep it.
Your gift
Goes straight into books, journals, pens and packaging bought from women-owned makers and bookshops.
Recurring gifts
If you chose to give monthly or annually, you can change or stop it any time — email us and we will handle it.

Stay With Us

We write when there is something real to say — a box that went out, a club that formed, a maker we found.
