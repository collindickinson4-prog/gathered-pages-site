Connecting Women
Through Shared Stories

Through free book club experiences, Gathered Pages brings women together to share stories, exchange perspectives, and build community—one page and one conversation at a time.

What we send

What’s in a Book Club Box

Each Book Club Box holds ten individual kits — one for every woman in the group, hers to keep.

The book
One copy per woman, chosen for the group and hers to keep.
Reading essentials
Journals, pens, bookmarks, and other thoughtful extras all purchased from women-owned businesses.
Reflection guide
A thoughtfully designed Reflection Guide encourages each woman to explore the story more deeply and connect its themes to her own life and experiences.
Facilitator guide
A welcome to read aloud, discussion questions and journal prompts, so leading the conversation takes no training and no prep.

Your Gift Gives Twice

Every dollar donated to Gathered Pages helps us purchase books, materials, and thoughtful extras from women-owned businesses whenever possible. Your generosity supports the woman who receives the kit—and the women whose creativity and work helped bring it to life. One gift creates connection, opportunity, and impact for women on both sides of the box.

Meet our founder

“In one particularly challenging chapter of my life, reading helped me recognize truths I had been avoiding and ultimately gave me the courage to make hard decisions that changed the trajectory of my life.”

Jamie Dickinson is a lawyer, a yoga instructor, a mother and a wife who has never been able to imagine her life without books. Gathered Pages Collective grew out of what she found when she started sharing them.
