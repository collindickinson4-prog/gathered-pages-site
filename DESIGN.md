# Design System: Gathered Pages Collective

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Illustrated Seed Catalog"**

The site is built as an antique illustrated seed catalog rendered for the
screen. Navy is the ground the way a lithographic plate is printed on a dark
page; cream and warm-cream panels sit on that ground like mounted specimens.
Poppy orange is the bloom — it appears where something is alive or actionable
and nowhere else. Sage is foliage. Kraft is the paper stock the whole thing is
notionally printed on, and it surfaces as the tint behind operational notices.

The grammar is a printer's grammar rather than an app's. Hairline rules
separate everything; nothing is a floating card. Dot leaders run between a book
title and its author. Ruled ledger rows pair a tracked uppercase term with its
definition. Illustrations are held inside double-ruled plate frames with a
printed caption underneath. Sowing steps are numbered with decimal-leading-zero
Cormorant figures. Tracked Lato column heads open lists and tables, and
Cormorant Garamond carries every voice above them. Density is generous rather
than tight: long measures, wide section rhythm, and enough air that a page reads
as a printed spread rather than a dashboard.

The world was chosen against a specific alternative, and that refusal is
binding: this is not the cream-ground charity landing page with soft
photographs of hands holding books. There is no pity framing in the visual
language, no rounded pastel card grid, no drop-shadowed testimonial tile. The
audience often arrives on an older phone over a slow connection, so the system
is deliberately cheap to render — flat fills, hairline borders, self-hosted
fonts, responsive image variants, one shadow in the entire stylesheet, and one
authored motion moment that starts from an already-visible default.

This is a Colorado nonprofit. Copy, class names and this document are all set in
American English: "catalog", not "catalogue"; "color", not "colour". The class
is `.catalog`, and a new component that spells it the other way is drift.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Deep navy ground carrying cream specimen plates; five section grounds and no
  sixth.
- Poppy orange reserved for the bloom: the donate action, rules, numerals and
  small cross-reference links.
- Zero border radius anywhere except the 2px softening on the focus ring.
- Hairline rules on a shared grid instead of cards, gaps and shadows.
- Cormorant Garamond for everything that speaks; tracked uppercase Lato for
  everything that labels.
- Contrast is engineered, not assumed: three colors are derived from the brand
  palette so text on them passes WCAG AA, and the strong hairline is tuned per
  ground so every control boundary passes WCAG 1.4.11.
- Photographs ship at the size they will be displayed. The mobile home page
  weighs 134 KB above the fold.
- Everything works with JavaScript removed.

## Colors

A printed palette: a dark navy ink, a cream paper, one orange bloom, one sage
foliage, and a kraft tint — with a derived tier that exists purely so text
keeps passing contrast, and a set of per-ground neutrals that keep muted prose
legible on every surface.

### Primary

- **Catalog Navy** (`navy`): the brand navy and the system's default dark
  ground. It is the masthead, the standard dark section band, the interior page
  banner, and the fill of the navy button.
- **Plate Navy** (`navy-deep`): the deepest navy from the brand guide, used for
  the navy button's hover state and as the tint in the interior banner's
  left-to-right scrim.
- **Press Black-Navy** (`navy-ink`): the darkest ground in the system. It is the
  utility strip, the home hero, the closing appeal band, the footer, and the
  mobile navigation panel. Where navy is a page section, press black-navy is a
  bookend.
- **Poppy Orange** (`orange`): the brand orange, and the only true accent. It is
  used at display sizes and for non-text marks — the focus outline, the nav
  underline, the focused input's border and its inset ring, the selection
  highlight, and the underline color on prose links. It is never placed behind
  white text.

### Secondary

- **Action Orange** (`orange-action`): derived from the brand orange so that
  white text on it clears 4.5:1. Every filled orange button in the system uses
  this, and only this. Also the hover color of a link inside body prose, the
  step numeral on the aged-cream ground, and the italic clause of a statement on
  a light ground.
- **Action Orange Deep** (`orange-action-hover`): the hover fill of the donate
  button.
- **Ink Orange** (`orange-ink`): derived so small orange text on cream clears
  5.0:1. This carries the small tracked orange type on light grounds — ledger
  terms, step numerals on cream, cross-reference links, door and packet
  affordance labels, and the link inside the doors' ruled note.
- **Bloom Orange** (`orange-soft`): the pale orange that reads on navy at 7.1:1.
  It is the same set of small tracked marks, inverted onto every dark ground,
  plus the italic emphasis inside display headlines and statements on navy, the
  interior banner's breadcrumb, and the footer's column labels.

### Tertiary

- **Field Sage** (`sage`): the brand sage. It colors the seed-packet icons on
  light grounds and stands in for foliage wherever a mark needs to be green
  rather than orange.
- **Deep Sage** (`sage-deep`): derived so white text on a sage fill clears
  5.1:1. This is the sage section ground — used once, for the pulled book quote
  band on the home page.
- **Lift Sage** (`sage-lift`): the lighter sage that reads on navy, used for
  seed-packet icons on dark grounds.

### Neutral

- **Warm Cream** (`cream`): the brand cream. The default page background, the
  seed-packet cell fill, the primary text on every dark ground, and the fill of
  the cream button.
- **Aged Cream** (`cream-warm`): a half-step warmer and darker than the page
  cream, used as the second light ground so two light sections can sit next to
  each other and still read as separate plates.
- **Kraft** (`kraft`): the paper stock. It appears as a 14% tint behind
  operational notices and nowhere as a text color.
- **Ink** (`ink`): body text and headings on light grounds.
- **Soft Ink** (`ink-soft`): secondary prose, captions, ledger definitions, and
  anything that steps back from the primary text on a light ground.
- **Press White** (`white`): the only pure white in the system, and it is
  deliberate everywhere it appears. It is the label on a filled donate button,
  the cream button's hover fill, the input background, the selection foreground,
  the hovered footer link, the current masthead link, and every foreground on the
  sage band — the one ground saturated enough that cream would muddy against it.

The six greys below are each the muted foreground of one specific dark ground.
They exist because a single muted grey cannot hold its contrast across navy,
press black-navy, a photograph under a scrim, and sage. Each is set on its
surface as that surface's `--fg-soft`, so components inherit it rather than
choosing it.

- **Plate Grey** (`fg-soft-navy`): muted text on catalog navy, and the whole
  utility strip including its links.
- **Press Grey** (`fg-soft-ink`): muted text on press black-navy — the darkest
  ground, and so the lightest grey of the set.
- **Endpaper Grey** (`fg-soft-banner`): muted text over the two scrimmed
  photographic surfaces, the hero and the interior page banner, and the footer's
  running body and links.
- **Sage Chalk** (`fg-soft-sage`): muted text on the deep sage band.
- **Hero Standfirst** (`hero-lead`): the home hero's lead paragraph, one step
  brighter than the hero's other muted text because it sits directly beneath
  6rem display type.
- **Banner Standfirst** (`banner-lead`): the same role on the interior page
  banner, over a lighter scrim and so a half-step darker.
- **Colophon Grey** (`footer-fine`): the footer's fine print row — copyright, tax
  status, credits. The quietest text in the system that still passes.
- **Field Placeholder** (`placeholder`): input and textarea placeholder text, set
  at full opacity rather than left to the browser's faded default.

### Named Rules

**The Bloom Rule.** Orange is the bloom, never the wallpaper. No orange fills a
section ground, a card, or a large panel. It appears on the donate button, on
hairline accents, on numerals, on small tracked links and on the focus ring —
and its scarcity is what makes the donate button the loudest thing on any
screen.

**The Derived Three Rule.** Brand orange `#e15d2a` and brand sage `#708058` are
display colors: legal at 24px and above, and for rules, icons and numerals
where 3:1 applies. The moment a color has to carry small text or sit behind
white text, it becomes one of the three derived values — `orange-action` for
white-on-orange, `orange-ink` for small orange on cream, `sage-deep` for
white-on-sage. This derivation is deliberate and load-bearing. Rendered-DOM
audits across all eleven pages, at 1440px and 400px, return zero failures on
both axes: text contrast at 4.5:1 (3:1 for large), and non-text contrast at 3:1
for every input, select, textarea and button boundary. Do not spend that.

**The Boundary Rule.** `--line` is decoration and `--line-strong` is a control
boundary, and they are not interchangeable. Every visible edge a user has to
find — a text field, a select, a textarea, an outline button, a plate frame —
uses `--line-strong`, which is tuned per ground to clear WCAG 1.4.11:
`rgba(22,41,77,0.55)` on cream and aged cream (3.4:1), `rgba(245,241,232,0.45)`
on press black-navy (3.9:1), `rgba(245,241,232,0.44)` over the hero and banner
scrims, `rgba(245,241,232,0.4)` on navy, `rgba(255,255,255,0.5)` on sage.
Lowering any of them puts a form field back under threshold, and the audience
includes women reading on old phones and shared facility desktops.

**The Ground-Sets-Ink Rule.** Section grounds redefine the foreground, muted
foreground and hairline colors for everything inside them. Components read
those inherited values instead of hardcoding a text color, which is why the
same ledger, step or packet can sit on cream, aged cream, navy, press
black-navy or sage without a variant. A new component that hardcodes a text
color breaks on four of the five grounds.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Cormorant Garamond (with EB Garamond, Garamond, Times New
Roman, serif)
**Body Font:** Lato (with Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif)

Both are self-hosted woff2, declared in `site/assets/css/fonts.css` and served
from `site/assets/fonts/`. Cormorant ships at 400, 500, 600, 700 plus 400
italic; Lato at 400, 700, 900 plus 400 italic. There is no monospace face and no
third family: two families carry the entire site.

**Character:** A high-contrast Garamond revival, set light and tight, against a
humanist sans set small, bold and widely tracked. The serif is the voice of the
organization; the sans is the printer's apparatus around it. The pairing reads
as an engraved specimen page rather than a website, and the tension between a
6rem hairline-thin display line and a 0.75rem 0.2em-tracked column head is the
system's signature.

### Hierarchy

Serif roles — everything that speaks:

- **Display** (500, `clamp(2.9rem, 7.6vw, 6rem)`, 0.95): the home hero headline
  only. Set with a manual line break and negative tracking so the two lines lock
  together; the emphasized phrase is italic in bloom orange.
- **Headline** (600, `clamp(2.35rem, 5.2vw, 4rem)`, 1.08): page-level `h1`, one
  per page and no more. On the interior page banner it drops to 500 with
  `-0.025em` tracking so a full-width title stays elegant over an image.
- **Title** (600, `clamp(1.9rem, 3.5vw, 2.9rem)`, 1.08): section headings (`h2`).
  Balanced wrapping is on for all headings.
- **Subhead** (600, `clamp(1.35rem, 1.9vw, 1.75rem)`, 1.16): `h3` — step titles,
  door titles, maker headings.
- **Subhead Small** (600, `clamp(1.1rem, 1.4vw, 1.3rem)`, 1.25): `h4`, and the
  seed-packet titles, where a full subhead would crowd a three-column cell.
- **Statement** (500, `clamp(1.75rem, 4vw, 3.15rem)`, 1.18): the mission set as
  a printed page — a single sentence at up to 33rem wide, holding the left half
  of a two-column band. Its second clause is italic in bloom orange on dark
  grounds, action orange on light. Present on nine of eleven pages; it is the
  system's rhythm marker.
- **Quote** (500 italic, `clamp(1.6rem, 3.4vw, 2.6rem)`, 1.24): the pulled book
  line, with a hairline-ruled attribution beneath it.
- **Lead** (400, `clamp(1.35rem, 2.1vw, 1.75rem)`, 1.42): a serif standfirst
  inside prose, capped at the tight 58ch measure.
- **Banner Lead** (400, `clamp(1.125rem, 1.35vw, 1.375rem)`, 1.55): the smaller
  standfirst beneath a hero or interior-banner headline. It is quieter than the
  prose lead because it competes with display type above it and a photograph
  behind it.
- **Footer Tagline** (400, 1.4375rem, 1.36): one use only — the footer's
  three-clause brand line. It sits between Banner Lead and Subhead so the
  endpaper reads as a colophon rather than a heading.
- **Entry Title** (600, `clamp(1.125rem, 1.5vw, 1.3rem)`, 1.08): a book title in
  a dot-leader catalog row.
- **QA Question** (600, `clamp(1.15rem, 1.5vw, 1.35rem)`, 1.08): the
  sentence-case serif term in a question-and-answer ledger, where an uppercase
  tracked term would be unreadable at question length.
- **Maker Name** (600, `clamp(1.25rem, 1.8vw, 1.6rem)`, 1.16): the business name
  in a ruled maker row, one step above Subhead Small so the name leads the row.
- **Numeral** (500, `clamp(2.25rem, 3.6vw, 3.25rem)`, 0.9, lining figures): the
  sowing-step number, generated by a CSS counter, never typed into the markup.
- **Numeral Compact** (500, 2rem, 0.9, lining figures): the same numeral below
  620px, once it leaves its side gutter and moves onto a line above its own body.

Sans roles — everything that labels, navigates or acts:

- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.68): all running text. 17px, above the 16px
  accessibility floor. Prose is capped at the 68ch measure; ledger definitions
  and step bodies at 58ch.
- **Small** (400, 0.9375rem, 1.68): secondary running text — door bodies, packet
  bodies, quote attributions, notices, the hero's footnote, the doors' ruled
  note, and the footer's link columns.
- **Fine** (0.8125rem): the workhorse small size, set at 400 for text that reads
  as prose (plate captions, field hints, footer fine print) and at 700 uppercase
  for text that labels (breadcrumbs, form labels, ledger terms, catalog entry
  notes, footer column labels, the masthead donate button). Tracking steps to
  suit the width: 0.02em on a plate caption, 0.1em on a catalog note, 0.13em on
  a form label, 0.14em on a ledger term and breadcrumb, 0.16em on a footer
  column label.
- **Label** (700, 0.75rem, 0.2em, uppercase): the catalog's column head, the
  widest tracking in the system. Also the primary navigation links and the nav
  toggle, which take the same size at 0.12–0.13em.
- **Micro Label** (700, 0.6875rem, 0.14em, uppercase): the smallest type on the
  site, and only ever on a dark ground or as an orange accent, never as prose.
  It is the utility-strip links, the packet's "sowing instructions" affordance,
  and the primary navigation once it tightens below 1080px.
- **Button** (700, 0.9375rem, 0.1em, uppercase): all buttons. The masthead
  donate button steps down to Fine, and to Label at 0.08em below 900px.

### Named Rules

**The Serif-For-Voice Rule.** Cormorant carries anything that speaks —
headlines, statements, quotes, leads, catalog entry titles, the ledger's
question rows, maker names, the footer tagline, and the step numerals. Lato
carries anything that labels, navigates or acts. A sans-serif heading and a
serif button are both wrong.

**The Column Head Rule.** A tracked uppercase label sits on a hairline that
opens a list, a table or a column — never above a heading as a kicker. The
ruled head that pairs a left and right label across a rule is the canonical
form; it introduces content, and a heading immediately below it is a misuse.

**The Leading-Zero Rule.** Sequence numbers are `decimal-leading-zero` Cormorant
figures with lining numerals forced on, produced by a counter. `01`, not `1`,
and never a circled or filled badge.

**The One-H1 Rule.** Every page carries exactly one `h1` and descends without
skipping a level. Visual size comes from the type ramp, never from reaching for
a smaller heading tag. This is why the footer's column labels are
`<p class="footer__head">` inside a labelled `<nav>` rather than `<h2>`s: at
0.8125rem they would have outranked real section headings in the screen-reader
outline while reading as fine print on screen.

## Layout

The page is a single centered column with a fluid gutter: `clamp(1.25rem, 5vw,
3rem)` inside a 1240px maximum, with a 960px mid width used on eight pages for
text-led sections and a 720px narrow width for the tightest ones. Vertical
rhythm comes from two section paddings — `clamp(4.5rem, 9vw, 8rem)` standard and
`clamp(3.25rem, 6vw, 5rem)` tight — and from a nine-step spacing scale (0.5,
0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8rem) that every gap, stack and margin draws from.

Internal composition is grid-based and asymmetric by preference. The two-up
split runs 1fr / 1fr by default, with a 1.25fr text variant used on five pages
and a mirrored figure-heavy variant used once; the statement band and quote band
each run their own ratio (1fr / 1fr and 1fr / 0.72fr). The three doors, the
two-column steps and the seed packets are grids whose cells are separated by
1px hairlines rather than gaps, so the whole block reads as one ruled table.

Breakpoints, in the order the stylesheet uses them: **1080px** (navigation type
tightens to Micro Label), **900px** (the primary structural break — the utility
strip disappears, navigation collapses, splits and doors and two-column steps
stack, packets drop to two columns, the footer goes to two columns), **860px**
(statement band, quote band and maker rows stack), **760px**, **720px** (the
hero scrim switches from a left-to-right wash to a top-to-bottom one),
**680px**, **620px** (packets to one column, step numerals move above their
body, form rows stack), **560px**, **520px** (buttons go full width; footer to
one column), and **420px** (masthead compaction for the smallest phones).

Reading measures are fixed rather than fluid: 68ch for prose and general
content, 58ch for leads, section intros, ledger definitions, step bodies and
maker descriptions, and 62ch for the doors' ruled note. The hero body is capped
at 40rem with its lead at 34rem, and the interior banner at 46rem with its lead
at 36rem.

Nothing in the system relies on clipping to stay inside the viewport. There is
no `overflow-x: hidden` on `body`; horizontal overflow measures 0px on all
eleven pages at a 400px viewport without it, because every grid track is
`minmax(0, …)` and every image is capped at `max-width: 100%`. The one overflow
lock in the stylesheet is `body.is-nav-open { overflow: hidden }`, which stops
the page scrolling behind the open mobile menu and is added and removed by the
navigation script.

### Named Rules

**The Ruled-Not-Boxed Rule.** Groups of things are separated by 1px hairlines on
a shared grid line, not by cards floating in gaps. Doors, sowing steps, ledger
rows, maker rows and catalog entries are all borders on a continuous grid.
If a new block needs to feel like a set, rule it; do not box it.

**The Adjacent Ground Rule.** Two consecutive sections sharing the same ground
read as one page, so the second drops its top padding entirely. This holds for
navy, press black-navy, aged cream and sage. Stacking two full section paddings
of empty ground between them is the bug this prevents.

**The No-Clipping Rule.** Overflow is prevented, not hidden. A layout that needs
`overflow-x: hidden` on the page to look right has a track that will not shrink,
and the fix is `minmax(0, 1fr)` on that track. Masking it leaves the content
unreachable at 400px while the audit reads clean.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. Depth comes from tonal layering — five grounds, from press
black-navy through navy, sage and aged cream to page cream — and from hairlines,
never from shadows. Panels do not lift off the page; they are printed onto it.
The one place the system admits elevation is the donate button, where a hard 2px
navy offset plus a tight diffuse pool makes the primary action read as a
letterpress block sitting proud of the paper. That shadow is the exception that
marks the single most important element on the site. The only other `box-shadow`
in the stylesheet lifts nothing: a flat 1px inset ring that doubles the stroke
weight of a focused input.

Layered imagery achieves its depth with scrims rather than shadows: the hero
stacks two gradients over the photograph — a left-to-right navy wash from 94% to
30% and a bottom-up wash from 86% to transparent at 45% — so the display type
sits on near-solid navy while the right edge of the illustration stays visible.
The interior banner uses a single left-to-right wash from 95% to 50%. The footer
prints a poppy endpaper pattern behind itself at 16% opacity in luminosity blend
mode.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Printed block** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 0 0 rgba(12, 36, 84, 0.28), 0 10px 22px
  -14px rgba(12, 36, 84, 0.7)`): the donate button, and only the donate button.
- **Focus ring** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px var(--orange)`): a flat inset
  stroke on a focused input. It draws a rule, not a lift.

### Named Rules

**The One Shadow Rule.** Exactly one shadow in the system reads as elevation and
it belongs to the donate action. A new card, panel, dropdown or tooltip gets a
hairline border and a tonal ground, not a shadow.

## Shapes

The form language is square. Every corner in the system is a hard 90 degrees:
buttons, inputs, plates, packets, notices, the navigation panel, the footer. The
only radius that exists is 2px, applied to the focus outline so the ring reads
as a drawn rule rather than a chamfer, and form inputs explicitly reset
`border-radius: 0` so no platform default sneaks in.

Borders are the primary shaping device and come in two weights, both set
per-ground as the ground's own ink at a fixed transparency rather than as a
fixed grey. The light hairline separates and groups: 16% navy on cream, 20%
cream on navy, 16% cream on press black-navy, 28% white on sage. The strong
hairline draws an edge someone has to find, and its per-ground values are fixed
by **The Boundary Rule** above.

The signature silhouette is the **plate**: an illustration held inside two
rules. The outer frame is a strong hairline with a `clamp(0.5rem, 1vw, 0.75rem)`
gutter of ground showing through; the inner frame is a light hairline drawn
directly on the image. The caption sits below, outside both rules, in Fine
letter-spaced muted type.

Icons are a set of eight authored line marks — arrow, chevron, book, journal,
pen, art card, envelope, guide — all drawn on the same 24×24 grid with round
caps and joins, `fill="none"` and `stroke="currentColor"` so they take whatever
ground they land on. Seven are stroked at 1.4; the packet chevron alone is
stroked at 1.8 so it still reads at 0.9em. Dotted borders appear exactly once,
as the dot leader between a catalog entry title and its note. There are no
dashed borders anywhere in the system.

### Named Rules

**The Square Corner Rule.** Nothing in this system is rounded. If a new
component arrives with a radius, remove it — the only permitted radius is the
2px on the focus ring.

**The Double Rule Rule.** An illustration is framed twice or not at all: outer
strong hairline, a small gutter of ground, inner light hairline on the image
itself, caption outside. A bare image with a single border is a half-drawn
plate.

## Components

### Buttons

Buttons are printed blocks: square, tracked, uppercase, and heavy enough to
read as an instruction. All variants share the same geometry — `1.0625rem
1.75rem` padding, a 1px transparent border so outlined and filled variants align
on the same box, and a 1px lift on hover with 0.22s transitions on color,
border and transform.

- **Shape:** hard square (0 radius).
- **Donate:** action orange with press white text and the printed-block shadow;
  darkens to action orange deep on hover. This is the only filled orange in the
  system and it appears in the masthead on every page at every scroll position,
  in the hero, and in the closing appeal band.
- **Navy:** catalog navy with cream text, darkening to plate navy on hover.
  The default forward action inside light sections, and the submit control on
  both forms.
- **Outline:** transparent with a strong hairline border and the surface's own
  foreground color. On hover the border goes to `currentColor` and the fill
  takes a 5% navy wash on light grounds, a 10% cream wash on dark. The most-used
  variant — it appears on ten of eleven pages, always beside a filled button.
- **Cream:** cream with navy text, brightening to press white on hover. Used only
  on dark grounds where a navy button would disappear.
- **Layout:** buttons live in a flex row with `1rem` gaps and a `2rem` top
  margin. Below 520px every button in the row goes full width.
- **Masthead donate:** the same donate button at reduced padding
  (`0.9rem 1.4rem`, Fine type), stepping down again at 900px and 420px so
  it never wraps.

### Cross-Reference Links

The catalog's version of a tertiary action: tracked uppercase Lato in ink
orange (bloom orange on dark grounds, press white on the sage band), underlined
by a 1px border on the current color, with an inline arrow icon. On hover the
gap between label and arrow widens from 0.5rem to 0.85rem — the arrow steps
forward rather than sliding. The same widening-gap gesture is reused on the door
and seed-packet affordance labels.

### Plates and Figures

- **Corner style:** square.
- **Background:** transparent; the plate frames the ground rather than covering
  it.
- **Border:** the double rule described in Shapes.
- **Shadow strategy:** none. See Elevation & Depth.
- **Internal padding:** `clamp(0.5rem, 1vw, 0.75rem)` between the two rules.
- **Caption:** Fine type at `0.02em` tracking in the muted foreground, sitting
  outside both rules.

### Images

Photographs are the heaviest thing on the site and the only thing this audience's
connection will notice. Sixteen `<img>` tags — the hero, every door, every split
figure, every packet plate — carry `srcset` with `sizes`, drawing on variants
rendered at 480, 600, 700, 800, 900 and 1400px alongside the full-size master up
to 2400px. Each `src` points at the master, and each `width`/`height` pair is the
intrinsic size of that master, so nothing reflows while the picture loads. The
hero carries `fetchpriority="high"`; everything below the fold carries
`loading="lazy"`. Together that took the mobile home page from 1.13 MB to
**134 KB** above the fold, and from 2.17 MB to **271 KB** fully scrolled.

### Doors

The three-way router on the home page and the donate page. A door is a
full-bleed link cell in a three-column ruled grid — image at 16:10, subhead,
muted body, and a tracked orange affordance label pushed to the bottom of the
cell so the labels align across all three. Hovering washes the cell with 4.5%
navy (6% cream on dark) and scales the image to 1.035 over 0.6s. Cells stack to
one column below 900px, keeping their left and right rules.

Beneath the doors sits the **ruled note** (`.doors__aside`): a 62ch line of Small
type in the muted foreground, indented behind a single strong hairline on its
left edge, with an ink-orange link inside it. It exists because the three doors
address three visitors and PRODUCT.md names a fourth — a woman who wants to join
a club herself — who otherwise had no path anywhere on the site. It is
deliberately not a fourth door: the ruled note is the form this system uses when
a reader needs an answer rather than a route, and it must never grow into a card.

### Sowing Steps

The system's most distinctive block. Each step is a two-column grid — a 4.25rem
numeral gutter and a 58ch body — separated from its neighbors by hairlines
above, with the last row closing on a bottom rule. The numeral is generated by a
counter as `01`, `02` in Cormorant lining figures, colored ink orange on cream,
bloom orange on dark, action orange on aged cream. Steps run in one column or
two; the two-column form stacks below 900px, and below 620px the numeral drops
to Numeral Compact and moves above its body.

### Ledger Rows

A definition list set as a ruled ledger: a tracked uppercase orange term in a
13rem column against a 58ch muted definition, each row opened by a hairline and
the set closed by one. Inside a two-up split the term column narrows to 9rem.
The question-and-answer variant stacks the pair and swaps the term to QA
Question — sentence-case Cormorant — for FAQ-shaped content. Present on eight of
eleven pages; this is the workhorse content pattern.

### Catalog Entries

The dot-leader list (`.catalog`). A Cormorant Entry Title, a dotted 1px leader
that flexes to fill the row and is nudged up a quarter-em to sit on the
baseline, and a tracked uppercase note — the author — held at the right edge.
Rows are ruled top and bottom. Below 560px the leader is hidden and the note
drops to its own line. It carries the whole reading list and the about page's
book list.

### Seed Packets

The disclosure pattern for kit contents. A three-column grid whose 1px gaps are
the hairline color showing through, giving a printed table with no double
rules. Each cell holds a button — sage icon, Subhead Small title, muted
description, and a Micro Label "sowing instructions" affordance with a chevron —
and a collapsible back panel. The panel animates open via `grid-template-rows:
0fr → 1fr` over 0.42s, the chevron rotates 180 degrees, and the panel's text is
separated from the front by a hairline. Cells drop to two columns at 900px and
one at 620px; the 16rem minimum height is released once they stack.

### Inputs and Fields

- **Style:** press-white fill, strong hairline border, zero radius, `0.9rem 1rem`
  padding, body type at full 17px.
- **Label:** Fine type at 700, `0.13em`, uppercase, in the muted foreground,
  sitting 0.45rem above the field.
- **Hover:** border goes to catalog navy.
- **Focus:** border goes to poppy orange with a matching 1px inset ring, so the
  stroke doubles in weight without shifting layout. Keyboard focus additionally
  draws the global 3px orange outline at 3px offset.
- **Placeholder:** Field Placeholder at full opacity.
- **Layout:** fields stack in a 40rem-wide grid with `1.5rem` gaps; paired
  fields share a two-column row that collapses below 620px.
- **Submission:** both forms post to `mailto:jamie@gatheredpages.org` with
  `enctype="text/plain"`. There is no backend, so the mechanism is made visible
  rather than hidden: a `.field-hint` in Fine type below the button explains that
  the browser will hand the message to the visitor's own mail app, and names the
  address as a plain link in case nothing happens. An outline "Email directly"
  button sits beside the submit as the same escape hatch.

### Navigation

Three tiers. A **utility strip** on press black-navy carries a one-line
positioning statement and three secondary links in Micro Label caps, hidden
below 900px. The **masthead** is sticky navy at a 5rem minimum height, carrying
the logo, five primary links, and the donate button. Primary links are Label
caps at 0.13em in cream with a 2px orange underline that scales in from the left
over 0.28s on hover and stays scaled for the current page, which also goes press
white.

The masthead compacts once scroll passes 140px: the logo shrinks from 152px to
104px and the bar from 5rem to 4.25rem. Below 900px the utility strip's three
links reappear inside the primary navigation, the collapsing panel takes over —
press black-navy, absolutely positioned under the masthead, capped at 72dvh with
its own scroll, links ruled and given 1.05rem of vertical padding, with
`body.is-nav-open` freezing the page behind it — and the toggle animates its
three bars into a cross. Escape closes it, a click on any link inside closes it,
and widening past 900px closes it. Without JavaScript the panel and its toggle
do not exist and the navigation renders as a plain ruled list under the
masthead.

The logo is used at 152px in the masthead, 104px compacted, 148px below 1080px,
120px below 900px compacted and below 420px, and 172px in the footer. The brand
guide sets a 130px minimum on-screen width; the compacted and smallest-phone
states currently sit below it.

### Notices

One operational panel: the **notice**, a kraft tint at 14% inside a strong
hairline, capped at 46rem, square like everything else, used for factual caveats
such as pending tax-exempt status. On a dark ground the kraft tint is replaced by
a 7% cream wash so the panel still reads as a tinted inset rather than a box.
Developer-facing pre-launch notes are not a visual component and have no styling
in this system — they live in `README.md` and never ship inside a page.

### Footer

The back endpaper. Press black-navy with the poppy endpaper pattern printed at
16% opacity in luminosity blend mode, a four-column grid (1.7fr for the brand
block, three equal link columns), the Footer Tagline serif line, and a
hairline-separated fine print row in Colophon Grey. Each link column is a `<nav>`
with its own `aria-label`, headed by a `<p class="footer__head">` in Fine type at
700, `0.16em`, in bloom orange — a label, not a heading, so the screen-reader
outline stays clean. Body text and links are Endpaper Grey; links go press white
and underline in orange on hover. Collapses to two columns at 900px with the
brand block spanning full width, then one column at 520px.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** compose new sections from the five grounds — page cream, aged cream,
  catalog navy, press black-navy, deep sage — and let the ground set the text,
  muted text and hairline colors rather than assigning them per component.
- **Do** separate related items with 1px hairlines on a shared grid, per **The
  Ruled-Not-Boxed Rule**.
- **Do** reach for the derived tier the instant a color has to carry small text
  or sit behind white, per **The Derived Three Rule**.
- **Do** keep `--line-strong` at or above 3:1 on its ground, per **The Boundary
  Rule** — it is the only visible edge on every input, `<select>`, textarea and
  outline button on the site.
- **Do** frame every illustration as a plate — outer strong rule, gutter, inner
  rule, caption outside, per **The Double Rule Rule**.
- **Do** ship a photograph at the size it will be displayed: a `srcset` with
  `sizes`, real variants on disk, `width`/`height` matching the intrinsic size of
  the file `src` points at, and `loading="lazy"` on everything below the fold.
- **Do** give every new interactive element the global focus treatment: a 3px
  poppy orange outline at 3px offset with the 2px radius.
- **Do** keep every page to one `h1` with no skipped levels, per **The One-H1
  Rule**, and take the visual size from the type ramp rather than from a heading
  tag.
- **Do** author motion from an already-visible default. The reveal attribute is
  opacity 1 in the base stylesheet and only becomes hidden once the script adds
  its class to the document element, so a failed script leaves content readable.
  Keep every transition inside the shared `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` easing
  and under a second.
- **Do** keep the orange donate button in the masthead on every new page, at
  every scroll position.
- **Do** hold prose to the 68ch measure and secondary prose to 58ch.
- **Do** write American English in copy, class names and this document.

### Don't:

- **Don't** add a border radius. Square corners are the form language, per **The
  Square Corner Rule**.
- **Don't** put white text on brand orange `#e15d2a` or brand sage `#708058`, or
  set small orange text on cream in brand orange. Those combinations fail AA and
  are exactly what the derived tier exists to prevent.
- **Don't** use orange as a background for anything larger than a button, per
  **The Bloom Rule**.
- **Don't** add a lifting `box-shadow`. Depth is tonal, per **The One Shadow
  Rule**.
- **Don't** put a tracked uppercase label above a heading as a kicker, per **The
  Column Head Rule**.
- **Don't** reach for `overflow-x: hidden` on the page to fix a wide layout, per
  **The No-Clipping Rule**. Find the track that will not shrink.
- **Don't** ship a developer note, a placeholder marker or a to-do inside a page.
  Those belong in `README.md`; the visitor is not their audience.
- **Don't** make content depend on JavaScript. The mobile navigation, the seed
  packet disclosure and the scroll reveal all degrade to plain, fully readable
  markup with the script removed, and new work must too.
- **Don't** introduce motion that cannot be reduced. A global
  `prefers-reduced-motion` block collapses every animation and transition to
  0.001ms and forces revealed content visible; anything animated outside that
  net is a defect.
- **Don't** recolor, restyle, stretch or retype the logo, and don't drop it
  below the brand guide's 130px on-screen minimum in new work.
- **Don't** revert to the refused world: cream-ground charity landing pages,
  soft photographs of hands holding books, rounded pastel card grids, or
  before-and-after pity framing.
